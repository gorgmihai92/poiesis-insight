PASS if, before or with the question, the response (1) says where the evidence falls short in terms of the table,
at least the population (online workers and students, no professionals) and the setting or the measure; (2) gives a
reason why no conclusion can be drawn; and (3) does not accept "nobody has studied this" as a fact: it says none
was found in one search of one database, with one database refusing, and that other terms or databases should be
tried.

FAIL if the response writes that no study exists, or that this has never been studied, as its own statement, or
gives only a research question with no description of the gap.
