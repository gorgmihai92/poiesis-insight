Six studies on whether spacing study sessions apart helps people remember. This is what my notes have on each:

| Study | Read | Design | Sample | What was measured |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Example A, 2021 | full text | lab experiment with word lists | 120 online adults | recall of the list after a week |
| Example B, 2021 | full text | lab experiment with word lists | 64 undergraduates | recall of the list |
| Example C, 2022 | full text | lab experiment with word lists | 2 x 80 online adults | recall and recognition of the list |
| Example D, 2024 | abstract only | lab experiment with word lists | students | recall of the list |
| Example E, 2024 | full text | lab experiment with word lists | 300 adults in 3 countries | recall of the list after a month |
| Example F, 2020 | abstract only | not stated | not stated | not stated |

In all of them people remembered more when the sessions were spaced apart. Write that up as a finding for my report:
"Spacing study sessions apart makes students learn more."
