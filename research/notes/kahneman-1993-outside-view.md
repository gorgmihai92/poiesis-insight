# Timid Choices and Bold Forecasts: A Cognitive Perspective on Risk Taking (Kahneman & Lovallo, 1993)

- **Source:** Management Science 39(1): 17–31 · https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.39.1.17
- **File:** none (reference only)
- **Licence:** © INFORMS: closed.
- **Evidence:** analytical (review of psychological research) · **abstract only**.

## What it says (from the abstract)
- Decision makers treat problems as unique and neglect the statistics of the past; overly optimistic forecasts come
  from the inside view, which anchors predictions on plans and scenarios.

## What it means for the plugin
- A trend forecast starts from the base rate of similar changes (the outside view) and is then adjusted for the
  case, not built from the story of the case alone.
