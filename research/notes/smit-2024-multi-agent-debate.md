# Should we be going MAD? A Look at Multi-Agent Debate Strategies for LLMs (Smit, Duckworth, Grinsztajn, Barrett & Pretorius, 2024)

- **Read:** abstract only, from the arXiv record · 2026-11-22

## What it says (from the abstract)
- Debate among several agents, in the forms they benchmarked, did not reliably do better than other prompting strategies such as self-consistency and ensembling.
- With tuning, some debate set-ups did better: the authors read this as debate being more sensitive to its settings, and harder to optimize, than the alternatives.
