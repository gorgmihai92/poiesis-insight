# Regression to the Mean: What It Is and How to Deal With It (Barnett, van der Pols & Dobson, 2005)

## What it says (from general knowledge, unverified)
- When units are picked because they are extreme on one measurement, they tend to be closer to the average on the
  next, with no intervention; it can be mistaken for an effect. Remedies include a control group and choosing units
  on a separate baseline measurement.

## What it means for the plugin
- Regression to the mean is a rival whenever units were selected for being high or low, or a change follows an
  extreme period.
