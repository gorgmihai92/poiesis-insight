# Causal layered analysis: poststructuralism as method (Inayatullah, 1998)

- **Source:** Futures 30(8): 815–829 · https://doi.org/10.1016/S0016-3287(98)00086-X
- **File:** none (reference only)
- **Licence:** © Elsevier: closed.
- **Evidence:** analytical (method) · **abstract or secondary summaries only**.

## What it says (from the abstract)
- Four layers: litany (the headline trend), social causes, discourse or worldview, myth or metaphor; moving between layers opens alternative futures rather than predicting one.

## What it means for the plugin
- A way to find uncommon conclusions: read the same data at the worldview and metaphor layers, not only the trend layer.
