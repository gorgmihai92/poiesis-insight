# The Sensemaking Process and Leverage Points for Analyst Technology as Identified Through Cognitive Task Analysis (Pirolli & Card, 2005)

- **Source:** Proceedings of the International Conference on Intelligence Analysis, 2005 (no DOI)
- **File:** none (reference only)
- **Licence:** conference paper: reference only.
- **Evidence:** empirical (cognitive task analysis, think-aloud protocols) · **abstract or secondary summaries only**.

## What it says (from the abstract)
- Analysis runs in two loops: foraging (seek, filter, read, extract evidence into a shoebox and evidence file) and sensemaking (schema, hypothesis, presentation), moving back and forth.

## What it means for the plugin
- The plugin keeps the two loops apart: evidence collected (from research or data) before schemas and hypotheses, and every hypothesis points back to its evidence.
