# Lacking Control Increases Illusory Pattern Perception (Whitson & Galinsky, 2008)

- **Source:** Science 322(5898): 115–117 · https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1159845
- **File:** none (reference only)
- **Licence:** © AAAS: closed.
- **Evidence:** empirical · six experiments; a later small replication (n = 28) did not reproduce the effect · **abstract or secondary summaries only**.

## What it says (from the abstract)
- People who lack control perceive more illusory patterns: images in noise, illusory correlations, conspiracies.

## What it means for the plugin
- Hidden-pattern work is exactly where illusory patterns appear: each pattern needs independent support and a test against chance before it is reported. The causal claim about control is contested.
