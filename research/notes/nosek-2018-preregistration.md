# The Preregistration Revolution (Nosek, Ebersole, DeHaven & Mellor, 2018)

- **Source:** PNAS 115(11): 2600–2606 · https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1708274114
- **File:** none (reference only)
- **Licence:** © the authors, PNAS licence: closed.
- **Evidence:** analytical (methods perspective) · **abstract only**.

## What it says (from the abstract)
- Generating hypotheses from existing observations (postdiction) and testing them on new observations (prediction)
  are different; mistaking the first for the second reduces the credibility of findings, and hindsight bias makes
  the mistake hard to avoid.
- Defining the questions and the analysis before seeing the outcomes separates the two; there are strategies for
  when the data already exist.

## What it means for the plugin
- A free search is allowed, but what it finds is a lead (postdiction). It becomes an insight only when it holds on a
  hold-out set aside before the search (prediction).
