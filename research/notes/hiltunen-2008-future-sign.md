# The future sign and its three dimensions (Hiltunen, 2008)

- **Source:** Futures 40(3): 247–260 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.futures.2007.08.021
- **File:** none (reference only)
- **Licence:** © Elsevier: closed.
- **Evidence:** analytical · **abstract or secondary summaries only**.

## What it says (from the abstract)
- A future sign has three dimensions: the signal (how visible it is), the issue (how widespread), and the interpretation (what it means to the receiver); weak signals are low on visibility and diffusion. The term weak signals comes from Ansoff (1975).

## What it means for the plugin
- Each signal is logged with visibility, diffusion and our interpretation, kept apart, so a weak signal is not mistaken for a trend.
