# Three horizons: a pathways practice for transformation (Sharpe, Hodgson, Leicester, Lyon & Fazey, 2016)

- **Source:** Ecology and Society 21(2): 47 · https://doi.org/10.5751/ES-08388-210247 (pages = PDF pages)
- **File:** `../papers/sharpe-2016-three-horizons.pdf`
- **Licence:** CC BY 4.0.
- **Evidence:** analytical (practice framework from facilitators' experience).

## What it says
- H1, the current way of doing things, losing its fit with emerging conditions; H3, an emerging pattern on the
  fringes that may succeed it; H2, the turbulent space of transitional innovations, some of which feed H3 (H2+) and
  some of which are absorbed back into H1 (H2−) (p. 5–6).
- Every pattern is present at all times, to a greater or lesser extent (p. 6).

## What it means for the plugin
- A structure for foresight: classify each signal as declining (H1), emerging (H3) or transitional (H2+ or H2−),
  instead of a single trend line.

## Quotes
- "each way of doing things is present at all times, albeit to a greater or lesser extent" (p. 6)
