# Identifying and Cultivating Superforecasters as a Method of Improving Probabilistic Predictions (Mellers et al., 2015)

- **Source:** Perspectives on Psychological Science 10(3): 267–281 · https://doi.org/10.1177/1745691615577794
- **File:** none (reference only)
- **Licence:** © SAGE: closed.
- **Evidence:** empirical · U.S. intelligence forecasting tournaments · **abstract or secondary summaries only**.

## What it says (from the abstract)
- The best forecasters kept their accuracy from year to year; their edge came from cognitive style, skill, motivation and teamwork, with forecasts scored against outcomes.

## What it means for the plugin
- Foresight claims become testable when written as probabilities with a date, then scored when the date passes.
