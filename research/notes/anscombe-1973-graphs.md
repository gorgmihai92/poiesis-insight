## What it says (from general knowledge, unverified)
- Four small data sets ("Anscombe's quartet") share nearly the same means, variances, correlation and regression
  line, yet look entirely different when plotted: a line, a curve, a line with one outlier, one point driving it all.

## What it means for the plugin
- Plot or tabulate before summarizing; a correlation or average alone can hide a curve, clusters or one outlier.
