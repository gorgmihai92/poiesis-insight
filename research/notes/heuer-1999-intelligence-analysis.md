- **Evidence:** analytical (practitioner synthesis of cognitive psychology).

## What it says
- Diagnosticity: evidence helps only if it changes the relative likelihood of the hypotheses; evidence consistent
  with all of them has little value, like a high temperature that says someone is ill but not which illness (p. 73, 129–130).
- People are poor at generating all the hypotheses; generate them with people of different perspectives (p. 124).
- Analysis of competing hypotheses, 8 steps (p. 125): list hypotheses; list evidence and arguments; judge
  diagnosticity; drop non-diagnostic evidence; try to disprove hypotheses rather than prove them; test how
  sensitive the conclusion is to a few key items; report the likelihood of all hypotheses, not just the most
  likely; name milestones that would show events taking a different course.

## What it means for the plugin
- An insight is stronger when the evidence behind it would not equally support the obvious alternative.
- Every conclusion states its alternatives, its few critical pieces of evidence, and what would show it wrong.
- See Dhami et al. 2019: the full matrix procedure itself did not improve accuracy.

## Quotes
- "Proceed by trying to disprove the hypotheses rather than prove them." (p. 125)
