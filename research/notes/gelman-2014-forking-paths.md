# The Statistical Crisis in Science (Gelman & Loken, 2014)

## What it says (from general knowledge, unverified)
- The "garden of forking paths": even without trying many analyses on purpose, the analysis chosen depends on the
  data seen, so many comparisons are implicit and a p-value overstates the evidence.

## What it means for the plugin
- Count and report every comparison tried in a free search, including the ones decided while looking; treat what it
  finds as leads until tested on the hold-out.
