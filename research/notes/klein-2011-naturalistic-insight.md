# A Naturalistic Study of Insight (Klein & Jarosz, 2011)

- **Source:** Journal of Cognitive Engineering and Decision Making 5(4): 335–351 · https://doi.org/10.1177/1555343411427013
- **File:** none (reference only)
- **Licence:** © SAGE: closed.
- **Evidence:** empirical · 120 insight cases coded on 14 features · **abstract or secondary summaries only**.

## What it says (from the abstract)
- Insights come from different paths: seeing a connection, noticing a contradiction, and escaping an impasse when a flawed assumption is dropped; a descriptive model unlike puzzle-based lab findings.

## What it means for the plugin
- The insight playbook looks for connections, contradictions and the assumptions behind an impasse, not only frequent patterns.
