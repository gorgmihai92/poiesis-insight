## What it says (from general knowledge, unverified)
- Control charts (Shewhart) separate common-cause variation, the usual noise of a process, from special-cause
  variation, a real change: points beyond limits set at about three standard deviations from the mean, or runs of
  points on one side of it, signal a change.

## What it means for the plugin
- A trend in time-ordered data is reported only if the change exceeds the series' usual variation; how many points
  the series has is said, and a short series gives a lead.
