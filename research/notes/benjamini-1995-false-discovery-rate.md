# Controlling the False Discovery Rate: A Practical and Powerful Approach to Multiple Testing (Benjamini & Hochberg, 1995)

- **Source:** Journal of the Royal Statistical Society B 57(1): 289–300 · https://doi.org/10.1111/j.2517-6161.1995.tb02031.x
- **File:** none (reference only)
- **Licence:** © Royal Statistical Society: closed.
- **Evidence:** analytical (proof and simulation) · **abstract only**.

## What it says (from the abstract)
- With many significance tests, controlling the expected proportion of false rejections (the false discovery rate)
  is an alternative to controlling the chance of any false rejection, with a substantial gain in power; a simple
  sequential procedure controls it for independent tests.

## What it means for the plugin
- A free search reports how many comparisons were tried and corrects for them with the false discovery rate before
  calling anything a lead worth testing.
