# Effect of scenario planning on field experts' judgment of long-range investment decisions (Phadnis, Caplice, Sheffi & Singh, 2015)

- **Source:** Strategic Management Journal 36(9): 1401–1411 · https://doi.org/10.1002/smj.2293 (pages = PDF pages)
- **File:** `../papers/phadnis-2015-scenario-planning-experts.pdf`
- **Licence:** author's manuscript: local only.
- **Evidence:** empirical · three field experiments with experts judging long-range investments (p. 1).

## What it says
- Using multiple scenarios did not raise or lower experts' overall confidence; judgments moved with how an
  investment fared in the scenario used; experts then preferred more flexible options (p. 1).
- Scenario planning has had little empirical testing (p. 1).

## What it means for the plugin
- Scenarios change decisions toward flexibility; they are not forecasts. Foresight output should say what holds
  across scenarios and what depends on one.

## Quotes
- "field experts prefer more flexible options to make specific long-range investments after using multiple scenarios" (p. 1)
